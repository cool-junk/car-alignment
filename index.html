Smartphone Alignment Setup Guide

1

Mounting Adapter / Jig

Place your phone in a flat alignment adapter frame, tripod plate, or hold it flush against the outer wheel lip or center hub cap.

2

Zero Offsets & Leveling

Before taking readings, calibrate the level on a known level surface or zero out vertical jig offset using the 'Zero Relative Tilt' button.

3

Steering Turn Sweeps

For Caster & Toe sweeps, use turn plates or low-friction tiles under front tires to ensure smooth turning without tire scrubbing strain.
